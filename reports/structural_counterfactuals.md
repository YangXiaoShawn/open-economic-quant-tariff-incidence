A record of what was deliberately not built, and why that is the correct outcome at this stage rather than a gap.

## Status: not implemented, deliberately

No structural module has been built, and therefore **no model-implied counterfactual or welfare number exists anywhere in this repository**.

The brief requires the reduced-form vertical slice to be stable and passing tests before the structural extension begins. It now is. The remaining blocker is different: a partial-equilibrium Armington or CES sourcing model calibrated on synthetic trade flows would produce counterfactuals with the full appearance of substance and none of the content. Sourcing shares, expenditure levels and the domestic alternative would all be simulated, so every simulated outcome would be a restatement of the generator's parameters.

Publishing that, however carefully labelled, would be the most misleading artefact the project could produce. It is therefore not produced. See docs/DECISION_LOG.md, D-017.

## What is already in place for it

- Country-specific sourcing shares, per product-month (`supplier_value_share`).
- Point-in-time tariffs by product, country and date, including baseline MFN separated from the additional duty.
- Pre-treatment expenditure weights immune to the shock.
- Tariff revenue, both as collected (`calculated_duties`) and as modelled by the policy engine, so the two can be reconciled.
- A reduced-form import-demand response that would supply an estimated elasticity with stated provenance rather than a borrowed one.

## What is missing

- Official trade flows (the binding constraint).
- A domestic-alternative expenditure series; without it the domestic nest of an Armington system is unidentified.
- Country substitution elasticities with citable provenance. Borrowing a number from the literature is acceptable **only** if it is reported as calibrated rather than estimated, in a table that separates data moments, estimated parameters, calibrated parameters and model-implied outcomes.

## Guard rail

The reporting layer blocks quantified welfare claims under **any** provenance, official included, until a structural module actually produces them. This is not a stylistic rule; it fails the build.
